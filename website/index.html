SKULLRUN

Escape the crypt. Outrun the Reaper.

One ordinary morning, the Reaper and his army of the dead took over the world. Now you're a skull, locked in a tomb deep underground... but somehow still awake. Push open the door, run, jump and dash through haunted lands, and escape before the Reaper notices you're gone.

Things that go bump

Skeleton

Walks back and forth. Jump on its head!

Ghost

Floats side to side. Can't be stomped.

Bat

Swoops up and down. Watch the rhythm.

Wraith

Wakes when you're close and drifts after you through walls.

Skull Spitter

Spits fireballs along the ground. Jump them, then smash it.

Witch

Throws smiley potions that go BOOM. Keep moving, or stomp her!

The Reaper

He's found you! Drifts after you through walls. Slow down and he'll catch you.

The Bone King

Throws bones, charges and slams the ground. Make him crash into a wall, then stomp his crown three times.

Mortis the Lich

Floats out of reach casting soul bolts and soul rain. When his magic runs out he sinks down: stomp him!
